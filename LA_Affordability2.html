Housing, food, energy and everything else: household budgets by income

Household income split four ways, housing, food, energy (home and car) and what is left for everything else, for every household in Los Angeles, Orange, Riverside, San Bernardino, Ventura and Imperial counties, by year and county income quintile. In 2024 the middle household across the region spent 23% of income on housing, 8.8% on food and 6.5% on energy, leaving 60%. For the lowest-income fifth the three costs exceeded reported income.

Data sources. Household-level cost-to-income ratios from the ACS 1-year PUMS, weighted by the household weight, summarized as medians and group totals by county, year and county income quintile. All dollar amounts are in the dollars of the survey year, so ratios need no inflation adjustment.
